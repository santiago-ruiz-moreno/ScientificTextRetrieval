# ScientificTextRetrieval
Advanced Information Retrieval
Our Contribution
Key contributions of our team included:
• Our task was mainly divided into finding the right approach, project methodology and coding.
The task was quite extensive and as a team we always had to have a plan A and plan B ready. While
Burak and Santiago Ruiz focused on the majority of the coding part, rest of us were constantly
working on the methodology and testing part along with making sense and relations among the
failed and succeeded results that we got. Burak’s extra hard work and expertise on coding made
this whole experiment a success.
Some of the highights of our work are listed below.
• Building a full retrieval and re-ranking pipeline from scratch, designed to handle scientific arti-
cles, and to find the most relevant ones for each query.
• We added a smart system to save results from different steps (like document rankings or translated
text), so we didn’t have to repeat the same work over and over. This saved us a lot of time when
testing different ideas.
• Integrating language handling and translation logic, this made our solution more flexible and
useful in real-world situations.
• Empirically validating performance trade-offs across abstracts vs. full-texts, and traditional vs.
neural methods. We tested different combinations, like using just abstracts vs. full documents,
and traditional vs. newer AI-based search methods. This helped us understand what worked best
and where improvements could be made.
